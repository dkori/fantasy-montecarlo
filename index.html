Fantasy Monte Carlo — Head-to-Head

Each player's fantasy-point outcome is a distribution reconstructed from sportsbook player-prop odds (de-vigged, Monte-Carlo-simulated) — not a single number. Pick two players to compare their full ranges, with Yahoo's point projection and your league's owner shown alongside. Use the owner dropdown to narrow the search to one manager's roster.
    Search and pick two players to compare head-to-head.

    1 · Density (PDF)

    The shape of each player's likely scores: taller = more likely, wider = less predictable. Best single view for separation.

    2 · Standard CDF

    The chance a player scores at or below any point total; a steeper curve = more predictable. Good for "will they clear my flex's floor?"

    3 · Points by percentile (flipped CDF)

    Read any percentile: the vertical gap between the two curves is how many points separate them at that outcome. Good for comparing floors (p10) and ceilings (p90) directly.

    4 · Difference (head-to-head)

    How many points the higher-projected player is ahead by at each percentile; where it dips below zero, the other player actually wins (e.g. a higher floor).

    Overlays: dotted vertical line = our simulated mean · dashed vertical line = Yahoo's projection (only for rostered players) · the owner badge on each chip is the league team that rosters them (blank = free agent).
    Built from one static JSON (no server). Projections come from a single book's de-vigged player props, 0.5-PPR, correlated Monte-Carlo. Free agents appear with our projection only (Yahoo doesn't project unrostered players). Kickers and defenses are not modeled.
